⚠️ Problem Statement

Problem: Many UPI users fall victim to fraud due to lack of real-time alerts and personalized risk detection, especially first-time digital payment users.


Required Solution: A platform that monitors UPI transactions in real-time, detects suspicious activity, and alerts users before confirming potentially fraudulent payments.

✅

Required Feature Status Implementation

Behavioural profiling for personalized fraud detection ✅ YES SenderProfile and ReceiverProfile tables track user behavior patterns
Risk scoring to delay, block, or flag risky transactions ✅ YES 3-tier risk scoring (SAFE/WARN/HIGH) with automatic blocking of HIGH risk

3-Stage Fraud Detection Pipeline

Google Pay style multi-layer protection system

STAGE 1: REFLEXES (Hard Rules)

54+ instant-check rules for severe violations. Examples: Velocity limit, Circular loop detection, Daily limits

STAGE 2: NETWORK (Social Graph)

Trust validation based on payment history. Stranger detection with spending limits. 2-depth friend-of-friend analysis.

STAGE 3: BRAIN (AI Analysis)

XGBoost + IsolationForest ensemble. 26-feature anomaly detection with adaptive learning from user reports.
